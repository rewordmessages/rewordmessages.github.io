Reword Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Reword is an Android app that rewrites the messages and emails you type, in the style and reading level you choose, using an AI service that you pick. It is made by Brian Vogel ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

In short: we don't have servers that receive your messages, and we don't collect, sell or share them. The text you ask to rewrite goes only to the AI you chose, only when you ask. There are no accounts, ads or analytics.

The text you rewrite

When you use Reword (the floating button, the Reword keyboard, the Quick Settings tile or the text-selection menu), the app sends that text, with your chosen style and reading level, to the AI you selected in the app's setup. A tap on the floating button puts the result straight into your message box, and you can undo it for a few seconds; a long press, and the other ways, show you the result first. Nothing is sent until you ask.

Where it goes depends on your choice:

  • On this phone: the text is rewritten by an AI model on your phone and never leaves it.
  • My own server: the text goes to the server address you entered, such as a computer you run. We have no access to it.
  • A cloud AI service (Claude by Anthropic, ChatGPT by OpenAI, Gemini by Google, OpenRouter, Groq, Grok (xAI), Mistral or DeepSeek): the text is sent directly from your phone to that company, using your own API key. That company's privacy policy and terms then apply to it, including how long it keeps the text and whether it may use it to improve its products. Some free tiers allow that, so check the terms of the service you choose. DeepSeek's servers are in China.

The app doesn't keep a history of your messages or rewrites. A rewrite only replaces the text in your message box, or is copied to your clipboard if you choose Copy. You decide whether to send it.

The Reword button (accessibility service)

The optional floating Reword button uses Android's accessibility feature (AccessibilityService API) to find the message box you are typing in, so it can show the button above it and put the rewrite back in. It works only in these texting, chat and email apps: Samsung Messages, Google Messages, WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, Messenger, Signal, Discord, Slack, Google Chat, Teams, Gmail, Samsung Email and Outlook. It reads the text in that box only when you tap or long-press the button. It does not read messages you receive, other parts of the screen, other apps, or password fields, and it is not used for any other purpose. The app asks for your agreement before the button can be turned on, and you can turn it off at any time in the app or in Android's settings.

The Reword keyboard and Quick Settings tile

The optional Reword keyboard is not a typing keyboard and doesn't record keystrokes. When you switch to it, it reads the text in the box you are editing so it can rewrite it, then switches back to your usual keyboard. The Quick Settings tile reads your clipboard only when you tap it, to rewrite what you copied.

Settings and API keys

Your choices, server address and any API keys you enter are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. They are not sent to us and are not included in Android's online backups. Each API key is sent only to the service it belongs to. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Reword Pro purchase

Reword is free. An optional one-time purchase, Reword Pro, unlocks extra styles. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your name, payment method or card details. Google Play tells the app only whether this Google account has bought Reword Pro. The app keeps that yes-or-no answer on your phone so Pro works without an internet connection, and checks with Google Play again whenever you open the app. If Pro is refunded, the Pro styles lock again. We don't receive, store or share any purchase information, and Google's privacy policy applies to your purchase.

The on-phone AI download

If you choose the on-phone AI, the app downloads the Gemma 4 model file (about 2.6 GB) from Hugging Face using Android's download manager. Like any download, Hugging Face receives your IP address and basic request details; see the Hugging Face privacy policy.

Reporting a rewrite

If you tap Report, the app opens an email to us in your email app, filled in with the rewrite, your original text and the settings used. Nothing is sent unless you send that email, and you can delete anything from it first. We use reports only to fix problems and prevent offensive results.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal information, and we don't have accounts.
  • We don't sell or share your information, and the app has no advertising or analytics.
  • We don't use your messages to train AI models.
  • Payments for the optional Reword Pro purchase are handled by Google Play, not by us.

Children

Reword is not directed at children under 13.

Security

Every connection the app makes to send your text is encrypted (HTTPS): to the cloud AI services and to your own server, which must have an https:// address. Your phone's lock screen protects the settings stored on it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's update notes.
