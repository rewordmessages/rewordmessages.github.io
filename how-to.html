How to use Reword

Updated October 28, 2026

Reword rewrites what you type, in the style you pick, right where you typed it. AI can make mistakes or even change the meaning of a message, so read the result before you send it.

Set it up (three steps)

  1. Connect to an AI. Open Reword and choose which AI does the rewriting: the free one on your phone (a one-time 2.6 GB download), your own computer, or a cloud service with your own key. Tap Test. More on this below.
  2. Turn on the Reword button. Reword uses Android's accessibility feature to show a ✎ Reword button above the message box in your texting, chat and email apps. Read what it can see, agree, then switch Reword on under Settings → Accessibility. It reads only the box you're typing in, never password boxes or messages you receive, and you can turn it off at any time.
  3. Try it. Use the Try it step in Reword, then type a message in any supported app.

The Reword button changes as you go

The button floats above the message box, and it changes shape so it stays out of your way:

  • ✎ Reword While you type, it shows its full name.
  • ✎ When you pause for a couple of seconds, it shrinks to a small round button on the top-right corner of the box, so it covers less of your message. It still works exactly the same: tap it to reword, long-press it for the panel. Start typing again and it grows back.
  • ✎ Rewording… While it works, it tells you it is rewording.
  • ↶ Undo Right after a tap rewrite, it becomes Undo for about 10 seconds. Tap it to put your original text back.

Use it

  • Tap ✎ Reword. Your message is reworded right in the box. For a few seconds an Undo button is there if you change your mind.
  • Long-press ✎ Reword to open the panel. You see the rewrite first and choose Replace to put it in the box, Try again for another version, or Cancel.
  • Nothing is sent until you press your app's own Send button.
  • A rewrite that came out wrong? Tap Report in the panel to send it to us.

Styles and reading levels

  • Open the panel and tap Change to pick a style and a reading level (Stupid, Simple, Everyday, College, Expert, Max and Max ×10).
  • Each style remembers its own reading level. Set Shakespeare to Expert and Friendly to Simple, and they stay that way when you switch back and forth. A style you haven't changed starts at Everyday.
  • Language: under the reading levels, pick the language the rewrite is written in, or leave it on Don't translate. Reword translates your message first, then writes it in your style and reading level, so rhymes and wordplay work in that language. It takes about twice as long. 17 languages: Arabic, Chinese (Simplified), Dutch, English, French, German, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Thai, Turkish and Vietnamese. Translating is part of Reword Pro, and one language is used for every style.
  • 8 styles are free. Reword Pro (one purchase, no subscription) unlocks 87 more, and translating.
  • Your favorite styles are buttons. The All styles pull-down lists every style, the free ones first and then the Pro ones grouped by kind. Press and hold any style in the list, free or Pro, to pin it as a button. Press and hold a button to unpin it. Every style always stays in the pull-down, so you can unpin one and still find it there.
  • The style and level you choose in the panel become your defaults, and the same choices are in the Reword app under Your defaults.

Other apps and other ways in

The button appears in Samsung Messages, Google Messages, WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, Messenger, Signal, Discord, Slack, Google Chat, Teams, Gmail, Samsung Email and Outlook. Anywhere else, use the Reword keyboard, the Quick Settings tile, or select some text and choose Reword. They are under Extras in the app.

Using the Reword keyboard:

  1. Turn it on once: open Reword, go to Extras, tap Turn on the Reword keyboard and switch it on in Android's settings.
  2. In any app, type your message with your usual keyboard.
  3. Tap the keyboard button at the bottom of the screen (on many phones a small keyboard or globe icon, near the bottom right) and choose Reword. If you can't find it, Reword's Choose keyboard button in Extras opens the same list.
  4. The Reword keyboard reads what is in the box (or only the text you selected) and shows the rewrite. Tap Change for a different style or reading level, Try again for another version, or Replace to put it in the box. It then switches back to your usual keyboard.

Choosing the AI

  • On this phone: free, private, and works with no internet after the one-time download. It is slower than the others, and works best on phones with 6 GB of memory or more. The on-phone AI is small. It does the everyday and character styles well, but many of the advanced wordplay styles work best with your own computer or a cloud AI. While On this phone is your AI, these are marked “Best with your own server or cloud AI” in the style list: Alliteration, Anadiplosis, Anthimeria, Antimetabole, Antiphrasis, Antithesis, Aposiopesis, Apostrophe, Assonance, Chiasmus, Consonance, Diacope, Double Entendre, Epanalepsis, Epistrophe, Epizeuxis, Hendiadys, Irony, Litotes, Malapropism, Metonymy, Oxymoron, Paradox, Parenthesis, Pleonasm, Polyptoton, Portmanteau, Pun, Spoonerism, Symploce, Synecdoche, Tautology, Tongue Twister and Zeugma.
  • Your own computer running Ollama, LM Studio or another compatible server. See the step-by-step guide.
  • A cloud AI with your own API key: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Grok (xAI), Mistral or DeepSeek. Several have free tiers.
  • You can pick a backup AI under Advanced, used if your first choice fails.
  • Every AI writes a little differently. Each one does some styles better than others. If a style doesn’t sound right, try it with another AI.

Tips

  • Write the message the way you'd say it. Reword fixes spelling and wording and keeps your meaning, names, numbers and times.
  • If a rewrite changes your meaning, tap Try again, pick another style, or lower the reading level.
  • Voice typing works well: talk your message, then tap Reword.
