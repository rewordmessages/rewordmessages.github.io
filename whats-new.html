What’s new in Reword

Updated October 29, 2026

October 29, 2026

1.0.40

  • If your main AI isn’t answering, Reword now uses your backup AI right away instead of waiting for the main one every time, and goes back to your main AI as soon as it answers again.
  • Rewrites by a cloud AI or your own server keep everyone your message mentions: “don’t tell mom” still mentions mom. “Be there in 10” stays 10 minutes from now, not 10 o’clock.
  • Translations keep “tonight” as tonight (Spanish “esta noche”, not “hoy”), and a time with no part of the day, like a meeting at 2:30, stays 2:30 instead of a guess like 14:30.

1.0.39

  • Translations keep the right time of day. “Be there by 7” on the way home stays 7 in the evening, written the way people normally write times in that language: 7 pm in English, 19:00 in German or Thai.
  • Thai and Persian (Farsi) added: 18 languages.
  • A 🌐 Language button next to Replace when you long-press Reword, so you can switch language in one tap.
  • A note says not every AI does every language and style well; if a translation is off, try another AI.

October 28, 2026

1.0.38

  • New for Reword Pro: a Language pull-down under the reading levels. Pick a language and Reword translates your message first, then writes it in your style and reading level, so rhymes and wordplay work in that language.

1.0.37

  • Apologetic no longer takes the blame for something the other person did. A complaint stays a complaint, said with a kind “sorry to bother you”, and you still apologize when it really was your mistake.
  • Apophasis, Paradox, Understatement, Sarcasm and Telegram keep your questions as questions.

1.0.36

  • Friendly and Professional keep your questions as the same question. “Why is it late?” still asks why, instead of turning into a statement or a request for an update.

1.0.33 and 1.0.34

  • Friendly is warmer: it turns a message that could sound rude into a kind one, while keeping what you meant and every fact.

1.0.32

  • New Pro style: Storybook Rhyme, a silly picture-book rhyme with a bouncy beat and made-up words.

1.0.31

  • When you choose your AI, a note says each AI writes a little differently and does some styles better than others. If a style falls flat, try another AI.

October 27, 2026

1.0.29

  • Pig Latin works with every AI, instantly, without asking the AI.
  • With Claude, the hardest wordplay styles get a little more care, so the rewrite comes back clean.
  • When you choose your AI, the On this phone note says which styles it does well.

1.0.28

  • The on-phone AI no longer adds questions you didn’t ask, like “Are you okay?”.
  • Most free styles and the character styles keep your meaning better on the on-phone AI: apologies, thanks and questions stay in.
  • Backwards Sage turns its sentences around more, the way a certain wise old master would.
  • Styles the on-phone AI does poorly are marked “Best with your own server or cloud AI” in the style list.
  • A style you haven’t used yet starts at the Every day reading level.

1.0.27

  • The on-phone AI starts loading while you type, even when the phone is low on free memory, so a rewrite takes about 3 to 4 seconds instead of about 12.
  • The on-phone AI stays loaded for 15 idle minutes instead of 5.
  • Try again can now give a different rewrite each time on the on-phone AI.

1.0.26

  • Rhyme, LinkedIn Speak, Noir Detective, Backwards Sage, Dysphemism, Sarcasm and Irony write better on the on-phone AI.
  • Pirate also says “savvy”.

October 26, 2026

1.0.25

  • The on-phone AI copies your message much less. It now rewords it, and tries again if its first answer was almost the same as what you wrote.
  • The on-phone AI follows the style more closely, including Simile, Haiku, Anaphora and Epizeuxis.
  • Pig Latin is now exact on the on-phone AI.
  • Poetic and Rhyme write the way they did before 1.0.24.

1.0.17 to 1.0.24

  • Each style remembers its own reading level. Set Shakespeare to Expert and Friendly to Simple, and they stay that way when you switch styles.
  • Questions stay questions in every style, even a short “ok ?” at the end of a message.
  • LinkedIn Speak now tells your message in its own words instead of quoting it.
  • A short notice and license when you first open the app: AI can make mistakes, so check each message before you send it. The button stays off until you agree.
  • A “How to use Reword” button in the app opens the how-to page.
  • Fixed a crash when changing the style from the Reword keyboard.
  • In the Pro style list, the character styles and the voices and formats (Poetic, Old English, Haiku and more) are now one group at the top.
  • The style pull-down is now called “All styles” and lists every style, the free ones first.
  • Any style can now be pinned as a button or unpinned, free ones too. Press and hold a style to pin it, press and hold a button to unpin it. An unpinned style is still in the pull-down.

October 25, 2026

1.0.8 to 1.0.16

  • More than 80 new Pro styles, in a pull-down grouped by kind (sound and wordplay, word order, repetition, meaning, rhetoric, voices and formats), 86 Pro styles in all.
  • Pin your favorite Pro styles as buttons next to the free ones: press and hold a style in the list to pin it, press and hold a pinned button to remove it.
  • New Poetic style (it keeps your meaning), plus Ancient Scripture and Old English styles.
  • Pirate and Rhyme are now free, so there are 8 free styles.
  • The style list opens centered on your current style, and the panel remembers where you were when you change settings.
  • A pull-down of the models on your own server.

October 24, 2026

1.0.1 to 1.0.7

  • Reword Pro, a one-time purchase with no subscription, unlocks extra styles. The first Pro styles were Valley Girl, Surfer Dude, Robot, Noir Detective, Medieval Knight and Backwards Sage. A “Have a promo code?” button is in the Reword Pro screen.
  • The Text and Email choice is gone. Reword keeps the layout of your message, so a greeting or sign-off stays, and none is added if you didn’t write one.
  • The AI on your phone is the default AI. Reword checks there is room before the 2.6 GB download, warns if your phone is short on memory, and asks before deleting the downloaded model.
  • Option to keep the on-phone AI loaded between rewrites, and to load it as soon as you start typing so the first rewrite is quicker. The default depends on your phone’s memory.
  • Reword tells you when an update is available in Google Play.
  • Grok (xAI) added to the cloud AIs.

October 23, 2026: Reword 1.0

  • The first Google Play version. Tap ✎ Reword to reword your message right in the box, with a few seconds to Undo. Long-press it to pick a style and reading level and see the rewrite first.
  • The button appears in Samsung Messages, Google Messages, WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, Messenger, Signal, Discord, Slack, Google Chat, Teams, Gmail, Samsung Email and Outlook. For other apps there is the Reword keyboard, a Quick Settings tile and a text-selection menu item.
  • Styles including Professional, Friendly, Concise, Formal, Apologetic, Shakespeare, Pirate, Rhyme, Cowboy, Street Slang, Southern, Gen Z and LinkedIn Speak, and reading levels from Stupid and Simple to Expert, Max and Max ×10.
  • Choose your AI: free on your phone (Gemma, no internet needed after a one-time download), your own computer running Ollama or LM Studio, or a cloud service with your own key (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral or DeepSeek), with a backup AI if the first fails.
  • A guided three-step setup, screen-reader (TalkBack) support, saved API keys encrypted on your phone, and a Report button to send us a rewrite that came out wrong.
