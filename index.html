Reword

Type it fast. Send it right.

Reword is an Android app that uses AI to transform your messy drafts into exactly what you need. Choose from many distinct styles—from professional and concise to fun personas like Pirate, Shakespeare, and Gen Z—or adjust the reading level from Simple to Expert. Whether you’re polishing a business email or just trying to sound like a 17th-century poet, Reword has a tone for it. Type or use voice to text like you always do, then tap ✎ Reword above the message box. Your message is cleaned up right where you typed it.

Reword is free to download and use. An optional one-time purchase, Reword Pro, unlocks 86 more styles. There is no subscription.

  1. Type your message in your texting, chat or email app.
  2. Tap ✎ Reword. Your message is reworded right in the box, with a few seconds to undo.
  3. You decide whether to send it.

Want a different style? Long-press ✎ Reword to pick a style and reading level and see the rewrite before it goes in.

See it in action

Works where you type

The Reword button appears in Samsung Messages, Google Messages, WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, Messenger, Signal, Discord, Slack, Google Chat, Teams, Gmail, Samsung Email and Outlook. In other apps, use the Reword keyboard, the Quick Settings tile, or select text and choose Reword.

Styles and reading levels

  • 6 free styles: Professional, Friendly, Concise, Formal, Apologetic and Shakespeare.
  • 86 more with Reword Pro (one purchase, no subscription, and new styles are added over time):
    • 13 characters and voices: Pirate, Street Slang, Southern, Cowboy, Gen Z, LinkedIn Speak, Rhyme, Valley Girl, Surfer Dude, Robot, Noir Detective, Medieval Knight and Backwards Sage.
    • 73 Advanced Pro styles in a menu, grouped by kind: sound and pronunciation (Alliteration, Spoonerism, Pig Latin and more), word order (Anastrophe, Tricolon, Zeugma), repetition (Anaphora, Epistrophe, Refrain), meaning and wordplay (Metaphor, Pun, Oxymoron, Hyperbole, Euphemism) and logic and rhetoric (Irony, Sarcasm, Rhetorical Question), plus voices and formats such as King James Bible, Sports Commentator, Movie Trailer, Haiku, Limerick and Telegram.
  • 7 reading levels: Simple, Everyday, College and Expert, plus Max, Max ×10 and Stupid for laughs.

Your choice of AI

  • On this phone: free, private and works offline after a one-time 2.6 GB download.
  • Your own computer running Ollama, LM Studio or another compatible server. Step-by-step guide for Windows 11 and Linux.
  • A cloud AI with your own API key: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Grok (xAI), Mistral or DeepSeek. Several have free tiers.

Private by design

  • Nothing is sent until you tap or long-press Reword, and then only to the AI you chose.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics.
  • The Reword button reads only the message box you're typing in, only in the apps above, and never password boxes or messages you receive.
