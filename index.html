Reword

Type it fast. Send it right.

Reword is an Android app that turns quick, messy messages into clear ones. Type or use voice to text like you always do, tap ✎ Rewrite above the message box, and tap Replace.

  1. Type your message in your texting, chat or email app.
  2. Tap ✎ Rewrite and pick a style and reading level.
  3. Check the rewrite, then tap Replace. You decide whether to send it.

Works where you type

The Rewrite button appears in Samsung Messages, Google Messages, WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, Messenger, Signal, Discord, Slack, Google Chat, Teams, Gmail, Samsung Email and Outlook. In other apps, use the Rewrite keyboard, the Quick Settings tile, or select text and choose Rewrite.

Styles and reading levels

  • 13 styles: Professional, Friendly, Concise, Formal, Apologetic, Street Slang, Southern, Shakespeare, Pirate, Cowboy, Gen Z, LinkedIn Speak and Rhyme.
  • 7 reading levels: Simple, Everyday, College and Expert, plus Max, Max ×10 and Stupid for laughs.
  • Text or email: short and natural for texts; greeting, paragraphs and sign-off for emails.

Your choice of AI

  • On this phone: free, private and works offline after a one-time 2.6 GB download.
  • Your own computer running Ollama, LM Studio or another compatible server.
  • A cloud AI with your own API key: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral or DeepSeek. Several have free tiers.

Private by design

  • Nothing is sent until you tap Rewrite, and then only to the AI you chose.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics.
  • The Rewrite button reads only the message box you're typing in, only in the apps above, and never password boxes or messages you receive.
